## Contact security

Contact [security@rox.one](mailto:security@rox.one). To report a vulnerability, include steps to reproduce it and avoid posting it publicly before a fix is available.

Read our [Privacy Policy](https://rox.one/privacy) and [Data Processing Agreement](https://rox.one/dpa).

## Self-hosting

Rox is [open source](https://github.com/macro-inc/macro) under the AGPLv3. For help running it on your own infrastructure, contact [self-host@rox.one](mailto:self-host@rox.one). Hosted-product certifications do not cover your own deployment.
